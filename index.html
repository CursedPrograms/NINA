NINA

Network Inspector & Notification Assistant

The fleet's caretaker. NINA watches every robot and tells you what's wrong, and why, before you have to go looking: who went offline, who rebooted, whose web page is so slow it froze her, and which mode change nobody made.

Overview

Every robot, looked after

Watch

The whole fleet

Every robot RIFT knows, checked every few seconds: answering or not, and how fast. Two misses in a row and she says it's offline; she says when it's back, too.

Diagnose

Causes, not just symptoms

Each problem comes with a likely reason: “NORA isn't answering — this PC is on another network”, or “NORA rebooted — check what was just switched on”.

Speak

Out loud

The important things are spoken with Windows text-to-speech and written to RIFT's mission log, so nothing slips by while you're working on a robot.

NORA

A closer look at the hub

NORA runs the network, so NINA watches her uptime for reboots, her drive mode and who changed it, and how long her web page takes to send.

Ports

Hands off the boards

Serial ports are listed, never opened: opening a port resets an Arduino and fights the program that owns it. She guesses which board each one is.

Fleet

One of the robots

She registers with RIFT like everyone else, so she's on the dashboard and in MILA's list, and she joins the Brainfuck conversations in her own pitch.

Quick start

Running in a minute

NINA runs on the PC next to RIFT. run.bat creates her venv the first time and starts her; run_all.bat in the GitHub folder starts her with the rest of the fleet.

> run.bat                  # sets up venv, then starts NINA
> options.bat              # her settings, as an options menu

# Her page: the fleet, and everything she's noticed
http://127.0.0.1:5012/

API

For RIFT and anything else

GET /status

Everything she knows: robots, NORA, this PC's WiFi, serial ports and recent events, as JSON.

GET /events

Her log, newest first: time, level (info / warn / problem), what happened and why.

GET /chirp?u=0-13

Says a Brainfuck phrase on the PC speaker, like the robots' buzzers. RIFT's conversations use it.

GET /ping

NINA alive, for anyone checking on the caretaker.
